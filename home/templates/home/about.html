Our Gym

At IronForge Athletics, we believe training should help you become stronger, fitter and more confident—whatever your starting point or goal. Our gym combines high-quality equipment, expert coaching and a welcoming community to create an environment where everyone can train with purpose.

Our Facilities

We provide a versatile training space with everything you need for effective, enjoyable workouts:

  • Strength and free-weight equipment.
  • Olympic lifting platforms and bumper plates.
  • Functional training areas for sled work, carries and conditioning.
  • Cardio equipment for structured and low-impact training.
  • Dedicated space for Pilates, yoga and mobility sessions.
  • Changing facilities and a friendly, supportive atmosphere.

Our gym is designed to support both independent training and coached sessions, so you can follow your own programme or benefit from the guidance of our experienced instructors.

Classes Types

Strength Training

Build a strong foundation with structured strength training focused on technique, progressive overload and full-body development. These sessions are suitable for beginners and experienced lifters alike, with exercises adapted to your ability and goals.

Hyrox

Prepare for the demands of HYROX with sessions combining running, functional movements and conditioning. Expect exercises such as sled pushes, sled pulls, rowing, farmer’s carries, wall balls and weighted lunges.

Green Zone Cardio

Improve your aerobic fitness through controlled, sustainable cardio sessions. Green zone training helps you build endurance while keeping the intensity manageable, making it an effective option for general health, recovery and long-term fitness.

Pilates

Improve core strength, posture, stability and body awareness through controlled Pilates movements. These classes are ideal for building a stronger foundation, improving movement quality and supporting your wider training.
